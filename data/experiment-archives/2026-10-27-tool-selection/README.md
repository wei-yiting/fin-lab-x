# Tool-Selection Analysis — 540 recorded runs scored against hand labels (30 rows × 6 configs × 3 runs)

> **One-line takeaway:** On 29 routing rows, `reader_luna_medium` and both `luna_medium`
> runs called every required source in 85–93% of runs. The Gemini configs did so in
> 47–61%. Over-calling is common in every config and mostly comes from the Finnhub
> quote. These are descriptive results from one annotator's labels; no paired test was run.

The scored runs are reused from two archives:
[`2026-10-22-model-reasoning-benchmark`](../2026-10-22-model-reasoning-benchmark/) (4 configs)
and [`2026-10-22-rag-bundle-ablation`](../2026-10-22-rag-bundle-ablation/) (2 configs). No
agent was re-run and no LLM judge was used.

## Claim boundary

- **Labels.** One annotator (the author) wrote all labels, before looking at per-config
  behaviour. The rules are in [`labelling_rules.md`](labelling_rules.md) (R1–R6) and the
  labels in [`labels.csv`](labels.csv). There is no second rater, so inter-rater agreement
  is unknown.
- **Recall is SEC-weighted by design.** SEC 10-K is 必要 on 25 of 30 rows. A recall gap
  between configs mostly measures whether the config reads the 10-K. The labels overrule
  the "best source" chosen when the questions were written on several rows; rows 19, 20 and 22
  were written as Finnhub questions and are now SEC 必要.
- **Over-calling is mostly the stock quote.** Under R2 the quote is 不必要 unless the
  question asks for the current price or market reaction. The quote accounts for the
  largest share of unnecessary runs in every config (see "Unnecessary calls by source").
- **Overlapping CIs are not a test.** The configs share the same rows, so a paired per-row
  contrast would be tighter. None was computed, and no difference here has been tested.
- **The two `luna_medium` runs are a replication check.** They are the same config run
  separately in two experiments, and they agree closely (recall 0.85 vs 0.86).
- **Row 26 hits the 10-call tool budget.** Its 必要 sources (financials, news, SEC) for 3
  companies take 9 calls. Every run also called the quote (可接受), and no run reached both
  news and SEC, and recall on row 26 is 0 for every config.
- **Row 29 Airbus news coverage depends on ticker naming.** The `N:AIR.PA` pair counts
  only when the call passes `AIR.PA`. Agents used `EADSY` or `AIR` instead, which are
  listed as off-list tickers. Those misses are about naming, not routing.
- **Coverage-gap checks rest on 2 rows** (row 4 full gap, row 29 partial). Their CIs are
  close to meaningless. "Insufficient evidence stated" is a zh-TW keyword heuristic
  calibrated on row-4 answers; read the audit table before quoting it.
- **Errors barely matter here.** Only 1 source use, across all runs, errored on every call,
  so recall counting all calls and recall counting successful calls only are the same to
  two decimals.
